
# Pakshi

development process of the Pakshi Mini Drone PCB, including component selection, schematic design, PCB layout,
and routing. Each phase records  challenges faced, and improvements made during the development process.

---

## Phase 1 - Component chose
**Time Spent:** 1 Hour

### Work Do
- selecting the required electronic components for the drone.
- Checked component availability and compatibility.

### result
Completed the initial component selection and prepared the required components for schematic design.

---

## Phase 2 - Schematic Design
**Time Spent:** 2 Hours

### Work Do
- Connect components in the schematic.
- Circuit wiring.
- Checked the schematic errors.

### result
Completed the schematic wiring and solve errors.

---

## Phase 3 - PCB Layout
**Time Spent:** 2 Hours

### Work Do
- Transfer schematic components into the PCB layout.
- place component on pcb

### result
Completed initial PCB layout with a custom drone-shape.

---

## Phase 4 - PCB Routing
**Time Spent:** 2 Hours

### Work Done
- Rout the PCB tracks between the components.
- Review the final PCB layout for improvements.

### Outcome
Completed PCB routing without errors .
